# Content Tag Runtime

Tags are bounded schema labels for registry organization.

Tag records share the global Content Registry namespace. Tags are validated like every other registry record and cannot carry forbidden execution, loading, service, client, save, analytics, telemetry, story, dialogue, or Chapter fields.

Tags are descriptive. They are never permissions to execute content.

## Hardening Notes

Tags are metadata, not gameplay behavior. Tags cannot carry forbidden final-content, loading, streaming, spawning, service, remote, client, analytics, telemetry, Workspace, or execution fields. A tag may help classify a schema; it may not command a future system.
