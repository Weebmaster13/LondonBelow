# Readiness Audit Runtime

`ReadinessAudit` records an audit pass over readiness metadata.

Fields:

- `auditId`
- `checklistId`
- `auditKind`
- `reviewer`
- `status`
- `findings`
- `tags`
- `metadata`

Audits are schema records only. They do not perform remediation, loading, mutation, or content application.
