# Asset Governance Certification Inspection Audit

Inspection audit records document copied inspection evidence review. They contain audit ids, inspection references, finding references, audit kind, reviewer, status, findings, tags, and metadata.

Audit records are review evidence only. Findings are reports only. Audits are not repair orders, authorization grants, execution commands, scheduler tasks, orchestration requests, persistence requests, networking requests, gameplay events, Presentation events, Save events, maps, rooms, dialogue, cutscenes, or Chapter content.
