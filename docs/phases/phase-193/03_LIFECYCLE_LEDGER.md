# Phase 193 - Lifecycle Ledger
## Ownership
Every completion connection is registered once and disconnected once. The ledger exposes created, disconnected, active, sorted active IDs, and balance status.
## Non-Ownership
The ledger is local diagnostics, not analytics or persistent accounting.
## Certification Boundary
Completion, cancellation, supersession, reconciliation, unmount, and shutdown must prove exact balance.
