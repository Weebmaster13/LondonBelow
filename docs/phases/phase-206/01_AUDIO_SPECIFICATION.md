# Audio Specification

Identity: "The Street That Listens".

Frequency philosophy:

- Preserve mid-range readability for footsteps, dialogue, ward feedback, and key interaction cues.
- Use low-frequency pressure as restraint, not a constant drone.
- Avoid harsh high-frequency rain masking during critical information.

Dynamic range:

- Silence and withdrawal are authored states.
- Threat and interaction cues outrank ambience and music.

Spatial rules:

- Audio may imply direction, floor, or occlusion category.
- Audio must not reveal exact coordinates or hidden AI state.

Certification:

- No final audio is certified until license, upload, permission, Studio audition, and user approval evidence exists.
