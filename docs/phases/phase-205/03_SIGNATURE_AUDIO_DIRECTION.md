# Signature Audio Direction

Audio identity: "The Street That Listens".

The street should begin as believable Victorian rain and distance, then gradually behave as if it is aware of the players. Phase 205 adds deterministic metadata and captions for this direction without claiming final audio implementation.

The five layers are:

- World bed.
- Local objects.
- Living world.
- Player physicality.
- Psychological pressure.

Original events:

- `impossible_footsteps`
- `wrong_bell`
- `source_less_carriage`
- `breathing_architecture`
- `constable_vale_presence`
