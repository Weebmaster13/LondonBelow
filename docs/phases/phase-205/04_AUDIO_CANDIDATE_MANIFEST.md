# Audio Candidate Manifest

`BlackwaterAudioManifest.lua` contains ten candidate entries, A1 through A10.

All entries are audition candidates only. They preserve source URLs, intended gameplay use, captions, accessibility equivalents, and blocked Roblox upload status.

No Roblox asset IDs are fabricated. Every candidate keeps `robloxAssetId` empty and `robloxUploadStatus` set to `assetUploadBlocked` until authenticated upload and experience permission verification occur.

Commercial game, film, soundtrack, and social-media audio sources are explicitly forbidden.
