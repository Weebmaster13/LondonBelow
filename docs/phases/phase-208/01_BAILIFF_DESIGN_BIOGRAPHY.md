# Phase 208 Bailiff Design Biography

The Bailiff was a borough debt officer preserved by Blackwater House after civic paperwork became a supernatural force. He judges because he still believes every trespass must be entered into the record before sentence.

His relationship to the ledger, wards, and Glass Heart is explicit in source configuration. The ledger functions as his court, the wards interrupt his authority, and the Glass Heart preserves testimony he cannot erase.

This biography guides runtime behavior and art direction. It is not dumped into player dialogue.
