# Interaction Runtime Audit

Phase 23 was audited as a server-authoritative interaction schema substrate.

## Certification

Interaction Runtime is schema-only. It records future interaction objects, eligibility, intents, locks, and cooldowns without final gameplay interaction, door/drawer/pickup execution, inventory ownership, animation, audio, UI, lighting, Workspace mutation, client authority, remotes, Monster AI, Narrative, Save, Horror pacing, or Chapter content.
