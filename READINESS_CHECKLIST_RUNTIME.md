# Readiness Checklist Runtime

`ReadinessChecklist` records the top-level readiness review for an asset and usage plan pair.

Fields:

- `checklistId`
- `assetId`
- `usagePlanId`
- `checklistKind`
- `readinessTier`
- `requiredGateIds`
- `findingIds`
- `decisionIds`
- `auditIds`
- `tags`
- `metadata`

Checklists are metadata only. They do not load assets or approve execution by themselves.
