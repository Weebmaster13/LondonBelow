# World District Runtime

District schemas describe high-level areas of London Below such as future exterior districts, undercity districts, or building-adjacent districts.

Required fields include `districtId`, `districtType`, `ownerSystem`, `regionIds`, `buildingIds`, `classification`, `metadata`, `context`, and `tags`.

Districts do not create Workspace folders, terrain, lighting, audio, fog, NPCs, navigation meshes, or Chapter layouts.
