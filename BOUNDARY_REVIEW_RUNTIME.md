# Boundary Review Runtime

`BoundaryReview` is the top-level proposed future asset execution boundary review schema.

Fields:

- `reviewId`
- `proposedRuntimeName`
- `assetId`
- `usagePlanId`
- `checklistId`
- `approvalId`
- `permitId`
- `gateId`
- `reviewKind`
- `reviewStatus`
- `reviewer`
- `riskIds`
- `requirementIds`
- `auditIds`
- `tags`
- `metadata`

Boundary reviews do not execute assets or grant client authority by themselves.
