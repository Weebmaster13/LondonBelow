# Readiness Decision Runtime

`ReadinessDecision` records a reviewer decision about checklist readiness.

Fields:

- `decisionId`
- `checklistId`
- `decisionKind`
- `status`
- `reviewer`
- `rationale`
- `tags`
- `metadata`

Decisions are not permission to execute assets. Future execution systems must separately validate their own contracts and Governance approvals.
