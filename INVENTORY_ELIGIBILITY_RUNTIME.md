# Inventory Eligibility Runtime

Eligibility records describe future conditions that may affect whether an item can be shown, used, transferred, inspected, or linked to another system.

This phase only validates and stores eligibility-shaped data. It does not evaluate chapter conditions, solve puzzles, unlock doors, trigger story reveals, or request horror pacing.

Eligibility payloads must be serializable, bounded, and free of client, remote, Workspace, execution, UI, Audio, Lighting, Monster AI, Narrative, Save, Chapter, story, dialogue, and cutscene fields.
