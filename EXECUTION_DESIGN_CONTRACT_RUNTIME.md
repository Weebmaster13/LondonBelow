# design contract Runtime

`ExecutionDesignContract` is the top-level proposed future Asset Execution Design Contract schema.

Fields:

- `contractId`
- `proposedRuntimeName`
- `assetId`
- `usagePlanId`
- `checklistId`
- `approvalId`
- `permitId`
- `gateId`
- `reviewKind`
- `reviewStatus`
- `reviewer`
- `responsibilityIds`
- `boundaryIds`
- `auditIds`
- `tags`
- `metadata`

design contracts do not execute assets or grant client authority by themselves.
