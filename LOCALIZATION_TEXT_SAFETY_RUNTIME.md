# Localization Text Safety Runtime

Text safety rules are constraints, not censorship or moderation execution.

Text safety schemas require `textSafetyId`, `ownerSystem`, optional `schemaType = LocalizationTextSafetySchema`, safe metadata, safe context, and safe tags.

This runtime does not moderate players, rewrite story, translate content, censor final text, or execute presentation behavior.

Text safety rules are constraints for future localization workflows. They are not censorship, moderation, rewriting, analytics, or player-facing enforcement.
