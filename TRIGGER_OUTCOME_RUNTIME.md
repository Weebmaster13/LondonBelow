# TRIGGER OUTCOME RUNTIME

Trigger outcomes are possible future results such as Pending, Satisfied, Unsatisfied, Deferred, and FutureOutcome. They are not computed facts and never complete gameplay.

## Production Hardening

Production hardening: outcomes reject unsupported schema types, unsupported outcome kinds, invalid trigger references, unsafe payloads, computed result markers, gameplay result markers, execution markers, callbacks, services, remotes, and Chapter content.
